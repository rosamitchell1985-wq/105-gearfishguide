Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 5 October 2026

GearFish Guide is built for senior Australians, and accessibility is at the heart of the design.

What we've built in

  • Adjustable text size — use the A / A+ / A++ buttons in the top menu on any page; your choice is remembered.
  • High colour contrast between text and backgrounds throughout.
  • Large buttons and form fields, sized for unsteady hands.
  • Keyboard navigation and a "Skip to main content" link on every page.
  • Screen-reader support — descriptive image text, labelled forms, and clear page structure.
  • Plain English — short sentences, no jargon.

Feedback

If anything on this site is hard to read or use, please tell us — we genuinely want to fix it. Email hello@gearfishguide.website or call (07) 5550 2148.
