Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver to all Australian states and territories via Australia Post, including PO Boxes and parcel lockers. We currently do not ship internationally.

Rates

Over A$99FREE3–7 business days

Dispatch

Orders placed before 12pm AEST on business days leave our Gold Coast warehouse the same day; otherwise within 1 business day. You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

Delays

Occasionally Australia Post experiences delays, especially around Christmas and Easter. Delivery times are estimates and not guaranteed, but if a parcel is significantly overdue, call us and we'll chase it down for you.

Click & collect

Gold Coast locals can arrange free click & collect from our Burleigh Heads warehouse — phone (07) 5550 2148 to organise a time.
