Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Applies to gearfishguide.website

GearFish Guide ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

What we collect

  • Contact details — name, email, phone and postal address when you order, contact us, or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Order information — products purchased, amounts paid, and delivery details.
  • Technical data — browser type, device, and pages visited, collected via cookies (see our Cookie Policy).

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders, issue receipts, and provide support.
  • To send our monthly newsletter if you subscribed — you may unsubscribe anytime, in line with the Spam Act 2003.
  • To improve our website and guides.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Ever.

Payment information

Card payments are processed by PCI-DSS compliant payment providers. We never see or store your full card number on our servers.

Access and correction

You may request access to, or correction of, your personal information at any time by emailing hello@gearfishguide.website or calling (07) 5550 2148. We respond within 30 days.

Complaints

If you believe we've breached the APPs, contact us first so we can resolve it. You may also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.
